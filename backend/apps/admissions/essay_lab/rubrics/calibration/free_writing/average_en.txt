I do not like the new bus route to my school. Before, the bus stopped near our building, but now I have to walk fifteen minutes to the main road. In the morning it is cold and sometimes I am late for first lesson. My neighbour says it is better for the city because the buses are faster, but it is worse for old people like her. I think the city should ask the people who use the bus before changing routes. In the end, a small change can make daily life difficult.
