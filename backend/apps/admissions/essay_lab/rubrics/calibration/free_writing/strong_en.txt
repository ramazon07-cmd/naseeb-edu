My grandmother counts everything: stairs, bus stops, the number of times the neighbour's dog barks before it gives up. At first I thought it was a habit of old age. Then I sat next to her at the polyclinic and she whispered "forty-one" when the nurse finished calling names, and I understood that she counts because numbers do not change their minds. I want to say this is sad. It is not only sad, because she also counted my school lunches, and I have never gone hungry. I still cannot decide whether counting is how she worries or how she loves. I wrote down her numbers for a week and they are on my desk, eleven pages, not one of them explained.
