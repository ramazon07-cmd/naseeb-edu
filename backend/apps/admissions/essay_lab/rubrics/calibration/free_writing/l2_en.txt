Every morning my little brother ask me to tie his shoelace, though he can do it. I used to be angry. Then I notice that he ask only on the days when our father go to work in Russia and the house is quiet. He is eight years. I think the shoelace is not about the shoe. It is a way to ask me to stay one minute more near the door. Last Tuesday I tie it very slow, and he said nothing, and we both look at the lace.
