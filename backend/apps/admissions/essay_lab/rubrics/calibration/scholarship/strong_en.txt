Every Friday I take the 6:40 bus from Chust to the school in Namangan, because our village school has no physics teacher. Since September I have photocopied my notes and left them with my younger neighbour Madina, and four students now come to my yard on Sundays. I got the idea after I failed a mock exam question that I knew only because a cousin had once shown me, and I did not like that luck decided it. The scholarship's mission is to bring skills back to rural communities, and I have already tried a small version. I want to study physics education and open a Saturday lab in our school, starting with the one oscilloscope the school never uses. I do not know yet whether four students is a beginning or just a hobby.
