My goal is to become a nurse and work in a rural clinic. In my district there is one clinic for eleven villages, and my grandmother often waited there for hours. I volunteered at the clinic last summer, helping with registration and sorting papers. I learned how important it is to be kind to patients. The scholarship supports students who return to rural areas, and this matches my plan, because I want to return to my district after graduation. With this scholarship I could pay for my studies and focus on learning. I am hardworking and I received a certificate from the regional biology olympiad. I hope to become a good nurse and help people in my region.
