I want become agronomist and go back to my village in Khorezm. Our cotton field have salt problem and my father lose half harvest two years. I read on the phone about plant that can grow on salt soil, and last spring I plant it in a small corner behind our house, 12 square metres. Only some plants live. My father say it is wasting time, but he still water them. The scholarship want students who bring skills back to the village, and I want exactly this. With the scholarship I can study agriculture and test the plant on bigger land with my neighbour's field. I do not know if it will work, but I know it is better to try than to wait.
