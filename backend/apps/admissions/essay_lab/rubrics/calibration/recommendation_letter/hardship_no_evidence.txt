To the Admissions Committee,

I am writing to recommend Malika Karimova, whom I have known as her school counselor for three years.

Malika's life has not been easy. When she was in grade 8 her father lost his job, and the family went through a very difficult period. Her parents separated the following year, and Malika moved with her mother and two younger sisters into a one-room flat on the other side of the city. Her mother works long hours at a market, and Malika has had to take care of her sisters after school and to help with cooking and cleaning. The family often did not have enough money for basic things, and for several months they did not have heating in the winter.

In spite of all these difficulties, Malika continued to come to school. She has faced more challenges than most students her age, and she has shown great strength and resilience. She never complained, and she always kept a positive attitude. Many students in her situation would have given up, but Malika did not.

Malika is a hardworking and determined student. She is mature beyond her years and understands the value of education. She knows that a good education is the way to a better life for herself and her family. Her teachers say that she is a good student, and she has good marks in most subjects.

Malika wants to study economics so that she can help her family and her country. I believe that a scholarship would change her life and give her the opportunity she deserves. She has already overcome so much, and I am sure she will overcome any difficulty at university as well.

I recommend Malika Karimova with all my heart.

Sincerely,
School Counselor
