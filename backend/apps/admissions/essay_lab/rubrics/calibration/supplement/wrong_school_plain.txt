Westbrook University is the place where I want to spend the next four years. I first read about Westbrook's environmental engineering programme when I was in ninth grade, and since then I have followed the work of its water research group online.

In our village near Nukus, the water in the canal turns white with salt every August. Two summers ago I collected samples from three points along the canal and tested them with a cheap conductivity meter my uncle bought in Tashkent. The readings doubled from the first point to the last.

At Westbrook I want to join the first-year water lab and learn how to design a filter that a village like mine could build and maintain without outside help. I already know which questions I would bring on my first day.
