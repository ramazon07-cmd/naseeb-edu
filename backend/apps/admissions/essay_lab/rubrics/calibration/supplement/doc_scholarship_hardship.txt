My father lost his job at the cement factory in the spring of 2022, and since then our family has lived on my mother's wage as a school cleaner. We are five people in two rooms. The rent is nine hundred thousand som a month and my mother earns two million, so every month we count what is left after the rent, the gas and the bread. In winter the gas bill is more than the rent, and in January last year we slept in one room with the door closed to keep the heat in.

My father looked for work for eleven months. He travelled to Tashkent twice and came back both times with nothing. In the second month he stopped speaking at dinner, and I started to do my homework in the kitchen so that I would not have to see his face. My little sister asked why we do not buy meat any more, and my mother said that meat is bad for children in winter. I knew it was not true, and I did not say so.

I have good grades. I was first in my class in mathematics and second in physics, and I have a certificate from the regional olympiad. But I do not have a laptop, and I do my programming homework on my mother's old phone with a cracked screen. Last year I could not go to the olympiad in the capital because the bus ticket cost more than we had that week.

Every day I think about how unfair it is that I may not study only because of money. I am tired of being the person who cannot go. I would like the scholarship committee to know that my family is not lazy, that my father worked for fourteen years without a day off, and that we have done everything we could. I hope the committee will read my situation and understand how hard it is for us.
