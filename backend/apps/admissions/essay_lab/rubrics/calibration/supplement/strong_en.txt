Last spring our village water tank overflowed twice because nobody could see the level from the pump house. I built an alarm from a used phone, two wires and a float switch from the bazaar, and it broke in the first rain. The second version survived, and I still do not know why. That question is why I want Professor Hale's embedded systems lab at Eastfield: her group publishes sensor code that runs for years without a battery change, and I want to find out what my second version got right. In my first month I would ask to read the lab's field-failure logs. I also want to run the Eastfield Makers Saturday workshop for local schools, because the pump house is thirty kilometres from the nearest computer teacher.
