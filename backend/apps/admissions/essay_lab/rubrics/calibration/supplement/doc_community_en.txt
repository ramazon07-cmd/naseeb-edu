My community is my family and my mahalla. For four years I have looked after my grandmother, because she cannot walk. Every day after school we drink tea together, I give her her medicine and I read the newspaper aloud to her. On Mondays and Wednesdays I tutor my cousins Madina and Sardor in maths. Sardor used to struggle with fractions, and now he checks the exercises himself.

In our mahalla everybody asks after everybody else: our neighbours bring my grandmother plov every Friday, and we lend their children books. Belonging to this community taught me what responsibility is. Sometimes I am tired and wish nobody knew it, but when my grandmother smiles and says "barakalla", I forget everything. I still do not know whether this community holds me up or whether I hold it up.
