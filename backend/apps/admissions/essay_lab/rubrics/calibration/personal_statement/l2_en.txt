In my village near Bukhara there is no English teacher, so I learned from a broken phone and videos with no sound. My mother sell vegetables at the bazaar, and I stand with her on Sunday and talk to tourist, even when I make big mistakes. One German man laugh at me, then he stay and teach me the word "bargain". I wrote it on my hand. I was angry because he laugh, and also happy because I learn, and I do not know which feeling is more true. Now I translate for our neighbours when they go to the clinic in the city, 12 times this year. I want to study medicine so that nobody in my village need a child to translate for them.
