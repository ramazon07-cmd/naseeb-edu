Last year my younger cousin Aziza failed her math exam, and my aunt asked me to tutor her. For two months I went to their apartment on Tuesdays and Saturdays. At first I just solved the problems on the board and she copied them. She still failed the next quiz, and I realized that I was not really teaching her. So I started asking her to explain each step to me, and slowly she began to catch her own mistakes. In June she got a 4 out of 5. I learned that teaching is not about knowing the answer but about being patient. This experience made me want to study education, because I want to help students who feel left behind.
