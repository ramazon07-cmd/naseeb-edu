# Landing placement logos

The placement lists are maintained in `src/LandingPage.jsx`:
the first row contains universities; the second contains government scholarships
followed by international programs. Proper names are not translated.

## Sources added on 2026-08-28

These marks identify the listed destinations and programs, not a partnership or
endorsement. Public availability does not grant permission to use a trademark;
confirm the relevant institution's usage terms before publishing.

The existing university assets from this branch were retained. New assets were
downloaded from the sources below. Cincinnati, Drexel and Liverpool SVGs were
extracted from the public homepage markup. Northwestern's SVG was decoded from
the official stylesheet's embedded wordmark. No logo artwork was generated.
Logo images have no background plates. The strip follows the page theme, while
CSS contrast filters distinguish light-source and dark-source marks. Multiply
and screen blending suppress embedded white rectangles without flattening the
detail inside crests. The downloaded artwork is retained unchanged.

The EYUF lockup combines the foundation's own SVG symbol with its full name,
following the official website's symbol-plus-text treatment. Apple uses the
standalone academy mark, without a campus or partner university's branding.

Belt & Road is interpreted as the HKSAR Government Scholarship Fund scheme,
consistent with the Hong Kong destinations in the university row. No separate
programme logo was verified. Its entry therefore explicitly labels the regional
emblem as **HKSAR Government**, rather than presenting it as the scholarship's
own logo. The [government's scholarship announcement](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202511/08/P2025110800325.htm)
confirms the programme context. Confirm the intended scheme before publishing
if a different Belt & Road scholarship was meant.

The repeated visual copies are hidden from assistive technology; the accessible
lists announce each entry once. Hover pauses motion. Keyboard focus and reduced
motion expose a horizontally scrollable list without duplicate copies.
