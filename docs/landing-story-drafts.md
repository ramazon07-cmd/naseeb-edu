# Landing page student stories: drafts for approval

The stories on the landing page (`STUDENT_REVIEWS` in `frontend/src/LandingPage.jsx`)
are real quotes from real, named students, shown with their photos. The current
quotes talk about mentors. These drafts retell each story around the platform's
modules instead.

**Do not publish a draft until that student has read it and agreed to it.**
A quote someone did not say, published under their name and photo, is a fake
testimonial. Once a student approves (or edits) their draft, replace only the
`quote` field of their record. The name, photo and university stay as they are.

Each draft uses only facts from the student's original quote: their university,
field and scholarship. Nothing new is added.
